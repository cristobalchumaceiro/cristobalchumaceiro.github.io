About Me

I'm an MSc Computer Science graduate from Queen Mary University of London, where my recent work has centred on applying artificial intelligence and machine learning to solve real-world ecological, climatological, and environmental problems.

My undergraduate studies and career taught me project management, effective communication, and leadership skills. While I still rely on them every day, my current transition into research is driven by a desire to tackle complex, open-ended problems.

I enjoy grappling with difficult concepts that allow me to keep making a positive impact and meaningful contribution through my work.
